Rate guide

Understanding how diamond pricing works.

The same stone can carry very different price tags depending on where it is bought. Understanding these three layers helps you judge whether an offer is genuinely fair.

01

Wholesale Pricing

Rates apply when diamonds move directly from a source like ours to jewellers, retailers or informed buyers, with minimal markup layered on top. This is the closest a buyer can get to a stone's true trading value.

02

Bulk Pricing

When a buyer commits to a larger quantity, whether a matching set or a mixed parcel, the per stone cost typically comes down further. This suits jewellers building regular inventory.

03

Retail Pricing

What most consumers see in a showroom. It generally includes several layers of markup for distribution, overheads and margin, stacked on top of the diamond's underlying wholesale value.
